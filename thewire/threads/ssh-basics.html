SHELLBOUND member 16:17
connection issues are almost always one of three things. wrong host, wrong port, or you're not using the password you think you are. check those before anything else. once you're actually in, three commands get you most of the way: pwd tells you where you currently are ls lists what's in the folder you're standing in cd foldername moves you into it, cd .. moves you back up one level tab is your best friend. start typing a filename or folder name and hit tab, it autocompletes the rest. saves you from typos and from having to remember exact spelling. for copy and paste in an actual terminal, forget ctrl+c and ctrl+v, those do something else in most terminals. right click usually gives you a paste option, or if you're on windows terminal it's ctrl+shift+c and ctrl+shift+v instead.
